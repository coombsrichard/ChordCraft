Pop Piano Quick Reference

Zero sheet music required — memorize shapes & numbers

1. How to Spot Inversions Instantly

Root Position (1 - 3 - 5)

Evenly stacked ("snowman" shape). The lowest note is the chord name.

C Major: C - E - G
1st Inversion (3 - 5 - 1)

Big gap at the top (a 4th). The chord root is the highest note.

C 1st Inv: E - G - C
2nd Inversion (5 - 1 - 3)

Big gap at the bottom (a 4th). The chord root is the middle note.

C 2nd Inv: G - C - E
